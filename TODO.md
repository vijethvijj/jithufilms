# Website outcomes

- [ ] **Brand identity, responsive navigation, and footer.** The website is for **Jithu Films**, specializing in wedding photography, cinematic wedding films, pre-wedding shoots, portraits, engagements, and special events. Use a warm cream/ivory/beige/black palette with subtle champagne gold; `#F5F0E8` background, `#29251F` primary text, and `#B69A70` accent. Style it as a premium, minimal, artistic, cinematic wedding editorial with generous whitespace, large photographs, thin borders, refined transitions and subtle film grain; use elegant serif headings with Inter or DM Sans body copy; make the layout responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile. Avoid a generic template and use a visual identity inspired by camera lenses, film, wedding albums and cinematic storytelling. Navigation includes an aperture logo and working Home, About, Our Stories, Gallery, Films, Services and Contact anchors; it is transparent over the hero, changes to cream after scrolling, underlines on hover, indicates the active section, includes an animated mobile hamburger, and has a prominent “Book Your Date” button. Footer includes the logo, a short brand statement, quick navigation, Instagram/other social links, contact information, dynamically generated copyright year, a back-to-top button, and a small rotating aperture decoration. Use editable/unconfigured placeholders for studio details not supplied.

- [ ] **First-session camera-lens intro.** On first visit in a session, show full-screen warm cream with subtle grain and a thin central circle; animate rotating camera-aperture blades that open/close to reveal a glowing lens; reveal “JITHU FILMS” letter by letter and the tagline “Every Frame Tells a Story.”; animate a 0–100% loading line synchronized with loading where practical; zoom through the lens into a wedding photograph and transition into the home page. Keep it about 3–4 seconds under normal conditions, do not unnecessarily wait for large images, include Skip Intro, respect reduced-motion settings, and do not replay on every internal navigation.

- [ ] **Interactive hero and photo explosion.** Full-screen cream hero has the label “WEDDING PHOTOGRAPHY & CINEMATOGRAPHY”, heading “Every Frame Tells a Story.”, supporting copy “We capture the emotions, the little moments, and the memories that last forever.”, buttons “Explore Our Stories” and “Book Your Date”. Show 6–8 distinct wedding photographs in an overlapping, slightly rotated stack with portrait, landscape and square frames and soft shadows. Pointer movement gently separates images in distinct directions based on their original positions; some move vertically/diagonally and rotate, grow slightly and gain depth; the photo closest to the pointer reacts most strongly; a subtle golden focus ring appears; photos smoothly return to their original positions on pointer exit. Movement is driven by pointer position with subtle parallax; touch devices use tap-to-spread instead of hover. Clicking any photograph opens a full-size lightbox with navigation controls. Keep photographs clear of the headline and buttons. Use generated distinct temporary images, with a central configuration for replacements.

- [ ] **About / studio story.** Use a split layout with a large behind-the-scenes photograph of a photographer using a professional camera. Include heading “Behind Every Beautiful Frame.” and the exact description: “At Jithu Films, we believe every wedding has its own story. From the quiet moments before the ceremony to the joy of the celebration, we capture every emotion through photographs and cinematic films.” Add highlights Candid Moments, Cinematic Storytelling and Timeless Memories. Reveal the image subtly when the section enters the viewport.

- [ ] **Shutter gallery, editorial grid, and lightbox.** Gallery begins with a large centered camera viewfinder showing a wedding photo, four autofocus brackets, focus/REC indicator, counter such as “01 / 08”, and a caption identifying the wedding story. Briefly pin only the gallery while visitors continue to scroll naturally: close shutter blades over the current image, change photograph while closed, reopen to reveal the next one, animate autofocus brackets and update the counter, and repeat for selected wedding photographs. After the last transition, expand the camera frame/photo outward, fade the frame border and reveal remaining images in an editorial/masonry grid before the gallery returns to normal scrolling. Use layered SVG/CSS masks and GSAP ScrollTrigger. Include large wedding portraits, couple photographs, ceremony details, candid family moments, engagement photographs and reception photographs. Each image has a subtle hover zoom and caption overlay. Clicking an image opens a full-screen accessible lightbox with previous/next/close controls, image caption, keyboard navigation and mobile swipe support. Gallery must remain usable if JavaScript effects fail.

- [ ] **Films, video modal and film strip.** Section heading is “Stories in Motion.” Provide 3–4 cinematic video cards for wedding highlights, pre-wedding films, cinematic trailers and traditional ceremony moments; each has a cinematic thumbnail, play icon, title, duration and elegant hover. Clicking a video opens an accessible modal that plays the corresponding working video with playback controls; closing it stops playback. Use local video files or working video URLs; do not invent nonfunctional links. Where actual studio reels are not supplied, sources must be clearly configurable and the UI must honestly identify an unconfigured/placeholder state. Add a horizontal strip of photographic frames that moves subtly with scroll.

- [ ] **Services and process.** Provide cards for Wedding Photography, Cinematic Wedding Films, Pre-Wedding Photography, Engagement Photography, Event Photography, and Albums and Prints. Each has a photograph or visual element, title, short description and “Enquire Now” link, with subtle reveal animations. Provide a horizontal four-stage timeline: **01 — Connect:** Understand the couple's vision; **02 — Plan:** Prepare the photography and filming schedule; **03 — Capture:** Document authentic moments and emotions; **04 — Relive:** Deliver photographs and cinematic films. Progressively animate the timeline as it enters the viewport.

- [ ] **Testimonials.** Provide a refined manually navigable, touch-swipe carousel with client quote, couple names, wedding/event type and optional small wedding photograph. Autoplay is optional and must not interfere with accessibility. Clearly identify demo/sample content; never present fabricated reviews as verified client testimonials.

- [ ] **Booking enquiry and contact.** Heading “Let's Create Something Beautiful.” The form includes full name, email address, phone number, event type, event date, event location, services required and message; validate required fields and show clear success/error states. Connect to the managed server/database, validate submitted data on the server and protect against spam. Only show success if the enquiry was actually inserted/sent; never display fake success. Include WhatsApp, email and Instagram actions and studio location if provided; use conspicuous configurable placeholders or honest unconfigured states when these details are unavailable. Document any provider setup still needed. The server endpoint only creates enquiries, returns no public enquiry list, and does not pretend that an email notification exists without configured delivery.

- [ ] **Performance, access and runnable handoff.** Use React with Vite and TypeScript, normal CSS or CSS modules (not Tailwind), GSAP + ScrollTrigger for advanced scroll animation, Lucide icons and HTML5 video; use Three.js only if a genuine 3D camera is necessary. Organize reusable components, data modules and hooks/styles; all imports/components must exist. Target smooth 60 FPS on supported devices, animate transforms/opacity, avoid expensive work on every scroll, clean up GSAP timelines and listeners, support reduced-motion, provide meaningful image alt text and visible keyboard focus, maintain readable contrast and prevent horizontal overflow, simplify cursor effects on touch, and provide animation fallbacks. Use a warm cinematic grade (natural skin tones, warm highlights, soft contrast, cream/gold accents; no excessive filters); optimize responsive image sizes and lazy-load, preloading only essential hero assets. Deliver complete source/CSS/dependencies, working interactions, setup/run commands, and clear instructions to replace demo photographs, video, studio details and social links. Keep `public/manus-routes.json` synchronized with the page routes. The website is a Preview handoff; do not publish without a separate request.
